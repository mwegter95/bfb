Meet Ashley

Hi, I’m Ashley Belisle!

I was raised in the northeast metro and graduated from Mahtomedi High School. After spending time in Northfield, Minn., Evanston, Ill., Minneapolis, and St. Paul, my husband Michael and I made Birchwood home in 2024.

As a member of the city’s Connections Committee, I have loved meeting my neighbors and learning more about the challenges in our city.

Outside of Birchwood, I lead a small but mighty marketing and communications team at a Minneapolis-based consulting firm. I also regularly volunteer with Second Harvest Heartland, the St. Andrew’s Community Resource Center, and my alma mater St. Olaf College. I enjoy a bike ride around White Bear Lake, a brewery patio, a good board game, and a good book – and I always love to hear about the best TV show you’ve been streaming lately.
